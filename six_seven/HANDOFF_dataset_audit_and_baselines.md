## 2. Ground-truth issue (tell Zang)
The official masks cover only **15.8%** of the pixels that really differ between authentic and tampered (per image: median 13%, max 30%).
Visible cause: the edit also regenerates the lookup code ("Mã tra cứu") and the **barcode**, which are not in the mask. So "authentic-looking" regions are labelled clean although they were changed.
Consequence: any detector that finds the barcode/lookup code is penalised as a false positive. I therefore evaluate against two GTs: `mask` (official) and `mask_diff` (all differing pixels dilated 2 px). Decide with Zang which one is the "truth" for the thesis, or document this as a limitation.
Positive-pixel prevalence is tiny (≈0.7% of pixels with the official mask) → pixel IoU/F1 at 0.5 are dominated by this; look at the oracle/dynamic F1 relative to the random baseline.

## 3. Baseline results (all 5000 images; tampered-only pixel metrics)
| model | GT | IoU@0.5 | F1@0.5 | F1 oracle-thr | F1 global dyn | det AUC |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ELA | official mask | 0.005 | 0.009 | 0.055 | 0.055 | 0.501 |
| random | official mask | 0.009 | 0.018 | 0.018 | 0.018 | 0.500 |
| ELA | mask_diff | 0.003 | 0.005 | 0.129 | 0.130 | 0.501 |
| random | mask_diff | 0.028 | 0.054 | 0.055 | 0.055 | 0.500 |
Per compression level (png/jpeg95/85/75/social_sim): ELA det AUC 0.489–0.516, i.e. chance everywhere (`results_baseline/*/summary.csv`).
Reading: (a) pipeline works end to end; (b) a classic forensic cue (ELA) cannot separate real from tampered here, which is expected for digitally rendered receipts with no camera/splice traces; (c) detection F1 0.667 is the trivial "everything is fake" score (balanced 50/50 set), so a real model must beat AUC 0.5 / F1 0.667, not just F1 > 0.
These are sanity baselines only. They say nothing about the SOTA models and must not be used to pick Scenario A/B/C.
